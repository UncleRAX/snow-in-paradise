Frequently Asked Questions

How do I purchase a ticket?

Select your preferred ticket category, copy the payment details, transfer the correct amount, and contact Paradise Circuit on WhatsApp with your payment receipt and attendee details.

Will my ticket be confirmed immediately?

No. Your booking is confirmed only after the team verifies your payment and acknowledges your purchase on WhatsApp. Keep your transfer receipt.

Where will the event take place?

The venue will be announced by Paradise Circuit. Please check the official event page and messages from our team for updates before travelling.

Can I buy a ticket for someone else?

Yes, but include the attendee's name and a working contact number when you message the team so the booking can be recorded correctly.
